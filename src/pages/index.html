Vimma

vimma (Finnish) a burst of eager, focused energy. The name

A browser for people who live in the terminal: Vim modes on every page, tmux-style workspaces and splits.

Your fingers already know the keys. Bare keys drive the page, ctrl+b drives tabs, panes and workspaces, and your hands never leave the keyboard. Firefox is underneath, so your sites and add-ons come along.

Why not just Firefox with an extension?

Extensions live inside the page. Vimma lives in the browser.

So the keys work where extensions can't, nothing steals them, and tabs, panes and workspaces answer to the same keymap.

What it does

  • Modal pages

    j k gg G / :

    Read, scroll, find and run : commands from the home row. Focus a field and you type as usual; Esc brings you back.

  • Link hints

    f F

    Every link, button and field on screen gets a short label, in frames from other sites too. Type it to follow the link, or to open it in a background tab.

  • Workspaces as containers

    ctrl+b ( ctrl+b ) ctrl+b w

    Keep work and personal apart. Each workspace is a Firefox container with its own cookies, logins, tabs, split and bookmarks folder, and all of it comes back after a restart. ctrl+^ toggles back, as Vim's alternate buffer.

  • Split panes

    ctrl+b v ctrl+b enter ctrl+b space

    Pages side by side. Move between them with ctrl+b h/j/k/l, zoom, swap and resize, and cycle tmux's layout presets, tiled and spiral included.

  • Status bar

    ctrl+b ?

    One line that always tells you where you are: mode, pending keys, workspace, tab 2/5, title, URL and find matches. The video above shows it at work.

  • Side panel

    ctrl+b b

    The workspace's tabs down the left of the window, there only when you ask. j/k and Enter to go, x to close, 1…9 to jump.

  • Bookmarks

    ctrl+b a ctrl+b shift+b

    Each workspace has its own folder: file a page as you type its path, then find it again in a tree you filter with /. They are Firefox's bookmarks, so Sync and your imports come along.

  • Add-ons on a key

    ctrl+b e :addon

    Open your password manager or ad blocker from the keyboard, and turn add-ons on and off, update or remove them without touching the toolbar.

  • Every key is yours

    config.toml

    The defaults are a starting point. Remap any binding and the ctrl+b prefix in ~/.config/vimma/config.toml; changes apply as you save. How.

  • Themes

    [theme]

    Tokyo Night by default, with any colour yours to change. It can also follow your desktop's theme. How.

  • Privacy defaults

    about:config

    No studies, no telemetry upload, no crash reporter, no sponsored content or promos. Each default is one switch away in about:config.

  • Firefox-compatible

    Firefox 157

    Built on Firefox's release channel, so the web works as you expect: sites see Firefox's user agent byte for byte, and add-ons treat Vimma as Firefox.

the name

Vimma is Finnish for a burst of eager, focused energy. You hear it in työvimma: a spell of work where everything flows and the hours slip by. It also happens to start with Vim. That is how browsing should feel: fast, fluent and absorbed.
