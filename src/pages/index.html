Vimma

vimma (Finnish) a burst of eager, focused energy. The name

A browser for people who live in the terminal: Vim modes on every page, tmux-style workspaces and splits.

Your fingers already know the keys. Bare keys drive the page, ctrl+b drives tabs, panes and workspaces, and your hands never leave the keyboard. Firefox is underneath, so your sites and add-ons come along.

What it does

  • Modal pages

    j k gg G / :

    Read, scroll, find and run : commands from the home row. Focus a field and you type as usual; Esc brings you back.

  • Link hints

    f F

    Every link, button and field on screen gets a short label, in frames from other sites too. Type it to follow the link, or to open it in a background tab.

  • Workspaces as containers

    ctrl+i ctrl+u ctrl+b w

    Keep work and personal apart. Each workspace is a Firefox container with its own cookies, logins, tabs and split, and all of it comes back after a restart.

  • Split panes

    ctrl+b v ctrl+b - ctrl+b z

    Up to four pages side by side. Move between them with ctrl+b h/j/k/l, then zoom, swap and resize, as in tmux.

  • Status bar

    ctrl+b ?

    One line that always tells you where you are: mode, pending keys, workspace, tab 2/5, title, URL and find matches. The session above draws one.

  • Side panel

    ctrl+b b

    The workspace's tabs down the left of the window, there only when you ask. j/k and Enter, or 1…9.

  • Every key is yours

    config.toml

    The defaults are a starting point. Remap any binding, the ctrl+b prefix and the space leader in ~/.config/vimma/config.toml; changes apply as you save. How.

  • Themes

    [theme]

    Tokyo Night by default, as on this page, with any colour yours to change. It can also follow your desktop's theme. How.

  • Privacy defaults

    about:config

    No studies, no telemetry upload, no crash reporter, no sponsored content or promos. Each default is one switch away in about:config.

  • Firefox-compatible

    Firefox 157

    Built on Firefox's release channel, so the web works as you expect: sites see Firefox's user agent byte for byte, and add-ons treat Vimma as Firefox.

the name

Vimma is Finnish for a burst of eager, focused energy. You hear it in työvimma: a spell of work where everything flows and the hours slip by. It also happens to start with Vim. That is how browsing should feel: fast, fluent and absorbed.
