Vimma: A browser for people who live in the terminal

Vim modes on every page, tmux-style workspaces and splits.

What it does

  • j k / :

    Modal pages

    Read, scroll, find and run commands from the home row. In a field you type as usual; Esc brings you back.

  • f F

    Link hints

    Every link, button and field on screen gets a short label, in frames from other sites too. Type it to follow the link, or to open it in a background tab.

  • ctrl+b w

    Workspaces

    Each one a Firefox container with its own logins, tabs, split and bookmarks folder, all back after a restart. ctrl+^ toggles to the last one.

  • ctrl+b v

    Split panes

    Pages side by side: move with ctrl+b h/j/k/l, zoom, swap and resize, and cycle tmux's layouts, tiled and spiral included.

  • config.toml

    Every key is yours

    Remap any binding and the ctrl+b prefix in ~/.config/vimma/config.toml; changes apply as you save. How.

  • :mcp on

    Local AI agents

    Claude Code, Codex and other agents can browse through MCP, confined to an agent workspace with its own logins. Every request asks you first. How.
