What it does

  • Modal pages

    j k gg G / :

    Normal mode scrolls, finds and runs : commands. Focus a field and you are in insert; Esc brings you back.

  • Link hints

    f F

    Labels every link, button and field on screen, frames from other sites too. Type a label to follow it, or open it in a background tab.

  • Workspaces as containers

    ctrl+i ctrl+u ctrl+b w

    Each workspace is its own Firefox container, with separate cookies and logins, its own tabs and split. All of it comes back after a restart.

  • Split panes

    ctrl+b v ctrl+b - ctrl+b z

    Up to four pages side by side. Move with ctrl+b h/j/k/l, zoom, swap and resize, as in tmux.

  • Status bar

    ctrl+b ?

    One line at the foot of the window: mode, pending keys, workspace, tab 2/5, title, URL and find matches. Like the one below.

  • Side panel

    space n ctrl+b b

    On demand, the workspace's tabs down the left of the window. j/k and Enter, or 1…9.

  • Omarchy theme

    omarchy-theme-set

    Takes its colours from your current Omarchy theme and recolours the moment you switch. Without Omarchy: Tokyo Night, as on this page.

  • Privacy defaults

    about:config

    No studies, no telemetry upload, no crash reporter, no sponsored content or promos. Each default is one switch away.

  • Firefox-compatible

    Firefox 157

    Firefox underneath, on the release channel. Sites see Firefox's user agent byte for byte; add-ons treat it as Firefox.

the name

Vimma is a Finnish word for a burst of eager, focused energy: the state of being on a roll. You hear it in työvimma, a spell of working when everything flows. It also happens to start with Vim. That is how browsing should feel: fast, fluent and absorbed.

status: pre-alpha

Not yet daily-driven, and no binaries yet: you build from source, on Linux x86_64. Version 0.1.0, on Firefox 157.0.1.
