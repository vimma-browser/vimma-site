Short answers to the questions that come up first. If yours is not here, the known limits may explain it.

First checks

  • Keys type instead of doing something? A text field has focus (the badge says INSERT). Press Esc.
  • Not sure what a key does? ctrl+b ? lists them all; / filters.
  • Your config.toml change did nothing? Look at the right of the status bar for a red error, or open about:vimma.
