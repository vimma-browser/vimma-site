In normal mode you move around a page with single keys: no mouse, no modifiers. These work on web pages, about: pages and in the PDF viewer.

Scroll

ToPress
Scroll down / upj or k
Scroll left / righth or l
Half a page down / upd or u, or Vim's ctrl+d / ctrl+u
A step down / up, as Vim doesctrl+e or ctrl+y
Top / bottom of the pagegg or G

A number before a key repeats it: 5j scrolls five steps.

Follow a link

  1. Press f. A label, one or two letters, appears on every link, button and field you can see.
  2. Type a label's letters. A link is followed, a button pressed, a text field focused.

F does the same for links only, and opens the one you pick in a new background tab, so you can pick several in turn. While labels show, Backspace undoes a letter and Esc cancels. Things you cannot see (off screen, or under a cookie banner) get no label: scroll first.

Find text

  1. Press / and type. Matches light up as you type, and the status bar counts them.
  2. Press Enter to stay on the match, or Esc to go back to where you were.
  3. n and N go to the next and the previous match.

A search typed in lower case ignores case; a single capital letter makes the whole search case-sensitive. ? searches backwards.
