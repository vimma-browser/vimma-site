Copy mode lets you select and copy text from a page without the mouse, with Vim's motions.

Copy some text

  1. Press v. A caret appears at the first text you can see, and the badge says VISUAL.
  2. Move the caret to where your text starts: j and k by line, w and b by word, or f and a character to jump to it on the line.
  3. Press v to start the selection, then move to extend it.
  4. Press y. The text is on the clipboard, and you are back in normal mode.

Esc leaves without copying. Selecting with the mouse works too: drag or double-click, then press y to copy, or use the motions to extend the selection.
