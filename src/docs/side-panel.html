The side panel lists the current workspace's tabs down the left of the window. It is closed until you ask for it, and closes again when you pick a tab.

Use it

ToPress
Open itctrl+b b
Move down / up the listj / k
Go to the highlighted tabEnter
Go to the tab in row 1 to 91 … 9
Look at the next / previous workspacectrl+i / ctrl+u (the panel stays open)
Close itEsc

While the panel is open, plain keys are the panel's; ctrl+b keys and : commands still work. A tab playing sound shows ♪.
