The side panel lists the current workspace's tabs down the left of the window, so you can see what is open and tidy it up in a few keys. It is closed until you ask for it, and closes again when you pick a tab.

Use it

ToPress
Open itctrl+b b
Move down / up the listj / k
Go to the highlighted tabEnter
Go to the tab in row 1 to 91 … 9
Close the highlighted tab (the panel stays open: x x x closes three)x
Mark or unmark the highlighted tabspace
Mark a range of rowsV, then j / k, then V
Reopen the last closed tabctrl+shift+t
Look at the next / previous workspacectrl+b ) / ctrl+b ( (the panel stays open)
Close it and go to the URL barI
Close it (with marks, the first Esc clears them)Esc

While the panel is open, plain keys are the panel's; ctrl+b keys and : commands still work. A tab playing sound shows ♪.

Show tabs together

  1. Open the panel with ctrl+b b.
  2. Press space on each tab you want to see side by side, for example your editor, the docs and a preview. Marked rows show ●.
  3. Press Enter. The marked tabs become the workspace's panes, in its layout, and no page reloads.

With a split already open, the panel opens with its tabs marked: unmark one and press Enter to take it out of the split, or mark another to add it.
