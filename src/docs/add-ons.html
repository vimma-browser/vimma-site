Your Firefox add-ons work in Vimma, and now they answer to the keyboard too. Open your password manager's vault, an add-on's options or its updates without reaching for the toolbar.

Open an add-on

  1. Press ctrl+b e (or type :addons). Every add-on you installed is listed, with its version and whether it is on.
  2. Move with j and k, or press / and type a few letters of its name.
  3. Press Enter: its popup opens with keyboard focus inside, so you can type at once. Esc closes it.

Faster still: :addon bw opens Bitwarden's popup by a few letters of its name, and Tab completes it.

Installing add-ons

As in Firefox: open the add-on's page on addons.mozilla.org and press its Add button. Vimma never installs anything by itself. You will not need Vimium or Tridactyl: hints, scrolling, find and the command line are built in, and a second set of keys would fight over the same keys.
