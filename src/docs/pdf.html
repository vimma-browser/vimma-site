PDFs open in Firefox's own viewer, pdf.js. Every Vimma key does there what it does on any page, and Vimma adds keys for pages, zoom and the outline on the keys it leaves free.

Read a PDF

  1. Open a PDF in its own tab. The status bar shows the page, such as page 3/12, and a PDF with an outline opens with it on the left.
  2. Press J and K for the next and previous page, or 12G to go to page 12.
  3. Press s to fit the page's width, or a to fit the whole page.
  4. Press / to find text; the status bar shows the match count.
