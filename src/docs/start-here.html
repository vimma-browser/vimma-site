Start here

Vimma is Firefox with a keyboard layer on top. Bare keys drive the page, as in Vim. ctrl+b, tmux's default prefix, drives tabs, panes and workspaces. These docs walk through it in the order you will meet it.

pre-alpha

Vimma is being daily-driven by its author before any release. There are no binaries: you build it from source, on Linux x86_64 only. What is written here works on main today; what does not is on the known limits page, and each topic links to its own.

Pick your path

  • New to Vim keys

    Read Modes and Moving around first. Five keys get you far: j k to scroll, f to follow a link, / to find, Esc to get back.

  • Coming from tmux

    The prefix is where you left it. ctrl+b c, n, p, v, z, w do what you expect. Start at The prefix and tabs, then Split panes.

  • Coming from Vimium or Tridactyl

    The page keys will feel familiar, and they also work in the URL bar, on about: pages and in the PDF viewer. What is new is the window: workspaces, panes and the status bar.

How keys are written

  • ctrl+b v: press ctrl+b, let go, then press v. The status bar shows prefix while it waits.
  • gg: two presses of g. G is shift+g.
  • <leader>n: the leader is space, so space then n.
  • A count before a key repeats it, as in Vim: 3j scrolls three steps.

Every key is yours

The defaults follow Vim and tmux, but none of them is fixed. Every binding, the ctrl+b prefix and the space leader can be remapped in ~/.config/vimma/config.toml, by the action names on the keys page. Save the file and Vimma picks it up; a mistake leaves your last working setup in place.

# ~/.config/vimma/config.toml
[keys]
prefix = "ctrl+a"         # the prefix, as tmux users often remap it
new_tab = ["prefix+t"]    # any action, any keys

The leader is leader under [modal]. The whole format is on the config.toml page.
