Start here

Vimma is a browser for the way you already work. Bare keys drive the page, as in Vim. ctrl+b, tmux's default prefix, drives tabs, panes and workspaces. Your hands stay on the keys, so reading, searching and switching context keep the same flow as your editor and terminal.

Pre-alpha

Vimma {{version}} is released for Linux x86_64 and for macOS on Apple Silicon: the install page has the one command for Arch Linux, the packages for other distributions and the Homebrew command for a Mac. Everything described here works in that release. What does not work yet is on the known limits page, and each topic links to its own limits.

What you see first

  • No tab strip. Vimma hides it. The status bar, the line at the bottom of the window, shows the mode, the workspace and which tab you are on (tab 1/1).
  • The start page, with the keys to start from: ctrl+b ? for every key, : for commands, f for link hints and ctrl+b shift+b for bookmarks.
  • The URL bar is focused, so you can type an address straight away and press Enter.

Your first five minutes

  1. Type an address in the URL bar and press Enter.
  2. Scroll with j and k. d and u move half a page; gg and G go to the top and the bottom.
  3. Press f: a label appears on every link. Type a label's letters to follow that link. H goes back.
  4. Press ctrl+b, let go, then press c: a new tab. ctrl+b n and ctrl+b p move between tabs.
  5. Click a search box and type: you are in insert mode, and keys type as usual. Esc leaves the box, and the keys are Vimma's again.
  6. Lost? ctrl+b ? lists every key; press / to filter the list and Esc to close it.

Pick your path

  • New to Vim keys

    Start with Modes and Moving around. Five keys cover most reading: j k to scroll, f to follow a link, / to find, Esc to get back.

  • Coming from tmux

    The prefix is the same ctrl+b: after it, c, n, p, v, z and w do what they do in tmux, and ( ) step through workspaces as they step through sessions. Start at The prefix and tabs, then Split panes.

  • Coming from Vimium or Tridactyl

    The page keys will feel familiar, and here they also work in the URL bar, on about: pages and in the PDF viewer. Firefox is underneath, so your add-ons come along, and now open with ctrl+b e. What is new is the window: workspaces, panes and the status bar.

How keys are written

  • ctrl+b v: press ctrl+b, let go, then press v. The status bar shows prefix while it waits.
  • gg: two presses of g. G is shift+g.
  • A count before a key repeats it, as in Vim: 3j scrolls three steps.

Getting around these pages

This site has a few keys of its own. Press ? for the list. In the docs, ] and [ go to the next and previous page, and / searches every page.
