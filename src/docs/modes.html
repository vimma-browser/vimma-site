Vimma's keys depend on the mode, as in Vim. Most of the time you are in normal mode, where letters are commands: j scrolls, f shows link hints. Click into a text field and you are in insert mode, where letters type. The badge at the left of the status bar always says which mode you are in, and Esc takes you back to normal.

The modes at a glance

BadgeWhen you are in itHow to get back to normal
NORMALOn every page, by default. Letters are Vimma's commands; the page does not see them.You are there.
INSERTWhile a text field has focus, or after i. Keys type into the page.Esc
HINTAfter f or gF: labels on everything you can click.Type a label, or Esc
ELEMENTAfter F and a label: a block of text is outlined, ready to copy (Copy mode).y copies and leaves, or Esc
VISUALCopy mode, after v: a caret and a selection (Copy mode).y copies and leaves, or Esc
COMMANDWhile you type a : command or a / search, at the bottom of the window.Enter runs it, Esc cancels
HELPWhile the ctrl+b ? key list or ctrl+b g tab search is open.Esc (or q in the key list)

Two kinds of keys

  • Bare keys (j, f, /) depend on the mode. In normal mode they are Vimma's; in insert mode they type.
  • ctrl+b keys work in every mode, even while you type in a field or the URL bar, because ctrl+b never types anything. Press ctrl+b, let go, then press the next key. The status bar shows prefix while it waits; Esc cancels.

Keys Vimma does not use reach the page as usual: a site's own shortcut on s or m still works in normal mode.

Typing in a page

  • Click a text field, pick it with f, or press i to go back to the one you were last in on the page, or the first (gi: the first one you can see; 3gi: the third). You are in insert mode.
  • Esc leaves the field and puts you back in normal mode. The page does not see that Esc. If the site needed it, to close a pop-up of its own for instance, press Esc once more.
  • On a page with its own keyboard controls, such as a game, i lets every key through to the page until you press Esc.
  • While you type, Vim's editing keys work: ctrl+w deletes a word, ctrl+u the line before the caret, ctrl+h a character. They work in the URL bar and the : line too.
  • I jumps to the URL bar from normal mode, its address selected, ready to type over. Esc brings you back to the page.

The command line

: opens a Vim-style command line at the bottom of the window. Type a command, press Enter to run it or Esc to cancel. Tab completes, and Up brings back what you ran before. Some to start with:

CommandWhat it does
:open example.comOpens the address in this tab; other words are a web search.
:tabnew example.comThe same, in a new tab.
:qCloses the pane, or the tab when there is one pane.
:workspace workSwitches to the workspace called work.
:helpLists every key, as ctrl+b ? does.

The full list is under Details. Any action on the keys page runs by its name too, such as :scroll_down.

If keys seem to do nothing

  1. Look at the badge. INSERT means a text field has focus: press Esc.
  2. Settings (about:preferences) focuses its search box when it opens, so it starts in insert mode: press Esc before j or k.
  3. Extension pages, data: and view-source: pages stay in insert mode: Vimma does not reach them. ctrl+b keys still work there. More on the troubleshooting page.
