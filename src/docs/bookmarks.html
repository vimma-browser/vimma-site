Vimma uses Firefox's own bookmarks, the ones you imported, synced or see in the Library, and gives them keys: one to file a page, one to open a panel where you find it again. Each workspace has its own folder of them.

Bookmark a page

  1. Press ctrl+b a. A prompt opens at the bottom of the window with the page's title in it.
  2. The bookmark goes into the current workspace's folder: in the work workspace, that is Bookmarks Toolbar → work. To file it in a subfolder, type the folder path in front of the title, separated by /, as in docs/My page. Missing folders are created, and Tab completes a folder name. A leading @ names another workspace: @shop/deals/Cart files it in the shop workspace's folder.
  3. Press Enter to save, or Esc to cancel.

A page that is already bookmarked shows where it is; edit the path to move or rename it. Renaming a workspace renames its folder; deleting one asks whether to keep, move or delete its bookmarks.

Find a bookmark

ctrl+b shift+b opens the current workspace's bookmarks as a tree on the left of the window, and follows you when you switch workspace (root under [bookmarks] in config.toml widens it to the whole toolbar, or to every bookmark). The quickest way to a bookmark is /: type a few letters of its title, address or folder.
