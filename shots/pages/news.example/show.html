Show: a browser you drive with hjkl

412 points · posted by modal_fan · 3 hours ago

I spend my day in Neovim and tmux, and the browser was the one place my hands had to leave the keyboard. So I built the browser I wanted on top of Firefox.

Normal mode scrolls the page with j and k, and f puts a short label on every link. ctrl+b is the prefix for tabs, panes and workspaces, as in tmux.

Each workspace is its own container, so work and personal logins never mix. Splits, zoom and resize work the way you would expect, and every key can be remapped in one TOML file.

It is pre-alpha and Linux only for now. Feedback is very welcome.
